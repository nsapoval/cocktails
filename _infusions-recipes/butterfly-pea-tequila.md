A simple infusion to turn your tequila blue or lightly purple for downstream use in color-changing (pH) cocktails.

---

* 250 ml blanco tequila 
* 5 g <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Sou-Zen-Butterfly-Antioxidants-Nootropics/dp/B07S4P9CBS/">butterfly pea tea</a>

Rinse a coffee filter with some water and put 5 grams of dried butterfly pea tea leaves into it. Pour tequila over the filter and strain. Repeat the infusion process 5-8 times until the liquid becomes intensely blue/purple. Aim for several shades darker color than in the finished product since ice and water will dilute the color you get. 

---

There is not much meaningful change in the flavor of the tequila just a change in color.
