This is essentially a rescaled Smuggler's Cove Hurricane meant to be served as a regular-sized cocktail. I also added a bit of Angostura to make this a bit more fun. As for the name, tropical depression is an early stage of a tropical storm and something that happens a fair bit in Houston.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1.75 oz Jamaican pot still black rum (Hamilton)</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz Jamaican rum (Smith & Cross)</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz lemon juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz passionfruit syrup (Smuggler's Cove specs)</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz 2:1 Demerara syrup</li>
    <li> 3 dashes Angostura bitters</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with ice and double strain into a chilled cocktail coupe. Serve up with an optional lemon wheel garnish.

---

Plenty of funk from the Jamaican rums, balanced with the acidity and sweetness of citrus and passionfruit. I like that this ends up being a typical cocktail sized drink, while bringing all the nice tropical flavors and exciting rum blend to shine. For a slightly more acidic version skip 1/4 of the Demerara syrup for an extra 1/4 of passionfruit syrup.

Rating: 9.5 / 10
