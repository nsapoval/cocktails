An original recipe inspired by the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/Surasang-Passion-Fruit-Honey-Puree/dp/B0DG61VL1W?th=1">honey passionfruit puree</a> I picked up at H-mart, and my lack of desire to spend a lot of time prepping ingredients for a passionfruit cocktail.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1 oz dark Bermuda rum (Goslings)</li>
    <li> 1.5 oz light rum (Flor de Cana)</li>
    <li> 0.5 oz lemon juice</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz lime juice</li>
    <li> 2 tbsp honey passionfruit puree</li>
    <li> 1.5 oz dry sparkling wine (optional)</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients except teh bubbles with ice and strain into a coupe glass. Top off with sparkling wine.

---

Overall a solid sour with nice passionfruit flavor and aroma. Works without sparkling wine top off, but you'll need to tune down puree to 1-1.5 tablespoons in that case. Using just lime juice might end up tasting a tad too harsh, but might be warranted with a bolder choice of rums.
