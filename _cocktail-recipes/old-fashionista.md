I am not the biggest fan of the Old Fashioned cocktail. However, recently I've been thinking about ways to reimagine Old Fashioned in a way that would encourage someone to start building up their bar. Making syrups -> a hassle, fresh fruit and handling peels -> a hassle, and so Old Fashionista was created.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1.5 oz Wild Turkey 101 Bourbon</li>
    <li> 1 oz Grand Marnier</li>
    <li> 5 dashes Angostura bitters</li>
</ul>

Combine all ingredients in a rocks glass, add ice, stir. No garnish.

---

This tastes very close to many Old Fashioned's you can find in various bars. Grand Marnier offers a longer tasting orange, but at the cost of the initial aroma punch you'd get from a fresh peel.
