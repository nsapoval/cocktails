If you never tried a <a hrer="https://jeffreymorgenthaler.com/flannel-shirt/">Flannel Shirt</a> by Jeffrey Morgenthaler you should probably start there. I wanted something that was a tad simpler, rum based, but still captured the cozy fall vibes.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1 oz amaro Averna</li>
    <li> 1 oz Jamaican pot still black rum (Hamilton)</li>
    <li> 1 barspoon 2:1 brown sugar syrup</li>
    <li> 3 dashes Angostura bitters</li>
    <li> apple cider to top off</li>
</ul>

Build in a Collins glass or a tiki mug. Swizzle with crushed ice then top off with some apple cider. We are not looking for a ton of apple cider here, I'd ball park 1.5-2 oz for starters.

---

Rating: 8 / 10
