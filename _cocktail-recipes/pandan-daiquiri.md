Daiquiri is one of my favorite cocktails, in part because of how versatile and different it can be. Agricole and pandan pair extremely well, so that's what I am going for here. 

---

<ul>
    <li> 1.75 oz rhum agricole blanc</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz Jamaican rum (Smith & Cross)</li>
    <li> 1 oz lime juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz [pandan syrup](/cocktails/syrups-recipes/pandan-syrup/)</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz Falernum (optional)</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with ice and double strain into a chilled cocktail coupe. 

---

Rating: 8.5 / 10
