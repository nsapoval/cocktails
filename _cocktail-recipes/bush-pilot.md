Another recipe from the <a href="https://www.wonkpress.com/products/minimalist-tiki">Minimalist Tiki</a> book. This time a variant on Jet Pilot.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1 oz Planteray O.F.T.D. Overproof rum</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz Planteray Stiggins' Fancy Pineapple Rum</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz aged Jamaican rum</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz falernum</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz lime juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz grapefruit juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz cinnamon syrup</li>
    <li> 4 dashes Herbstura</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with crushed ice (or flash blend). Pour into a footed pilsner glass. Top off with fresh crushed ice. Garnish with a mint sprig, pineapple wedge, and a cinnamon stick.

---

This cocktail fits my flavor profile better than the Smuggler's Cove version of Jet Pilot. It comes out sweet, strong, and spiced, yet manages to balance acidity really well. The drink feels rich without being overly syrupy, and a plenty of crushed ice makes it a pleasant sipper.

Rating: 9 / 10
