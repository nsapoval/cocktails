An absolute classic and an overall great cocktail! Slight change up from the Smuggler's Cove book recipe.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1 oz dark rum (Plantaray Original Dark)</li>
    <li> 1 oz Jamaican rum (Smith and Cross)</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz lemon juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz pineapple juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz orgeat</li>
    <li> 2 dashes Peychaud's bitters</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with crushed ice and open pour into a rocks glass.

---

I upped the orgeat to 0.75 oz instead of 0.5 oz in the Smuggler's Cove book (page for Humuhumunukunukuapua'a) since I like the taste of orgeat and how it blends with the rums. Somewhat compensating for extra sweetness with a full ounce of the S&C navy strength Jamaican rum.
