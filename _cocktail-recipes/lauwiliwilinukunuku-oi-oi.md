An absolute classic and an overall great cocktail! Posting the Smuggler's Cove book recipe below.

---

<ul>
    <li> 2 oz gin (Charing Cross)</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz lemon juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz pineapple juice</li>
    <li> 0.5 oz orgeat</li>
    <li> 2 dashes Peychaud's bitters</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with crushed ice and open pour into a rocks glass.

---

Great cocktail overall, similarly to Mai Tai I do not mind a touch more orgeat in this cocktail. However, the recipe as written works great.
