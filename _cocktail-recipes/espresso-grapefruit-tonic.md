Grapefruit cordial works great in a gimlet. I made the original one in the photo with a butterfly pea infused tequila, but a butterfly pea infused gin would work really well here too.

---

<ul>
    <li> 0.75 oz (25 g) <a href="https://nsapoval.github.io/cocktails/syrups-recipes/grapefruit-cordial/">grapefruit cordial</a></li>
    <li> 120 g tonic water</li>
    <li> 24 g espresso</li>
</ul>

Combine grapefruit cordial and tonic water in a glass and add ice, lightly stir. Top off with a shot of espresso and stir again to incorporate all ingredients.

---

I already like espresso tonics, so tossing a bit of grapefruit in there was a natural choice. IMHO cordial adds more grapefruit flavor and stands up better in a drink than grapefruit juice would. Using cordial also allows for making a much shorter drink by using 0.25 or 0.5 oz of cordial, about 25-30 g of tonic water and the same amount of espresso.
