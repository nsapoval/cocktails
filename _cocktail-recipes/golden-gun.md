The recipe from Smuggler's Cove book featuring the winning creation from Tiki Oasis 2012 seminar on exotic cocktails.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1 oz dark rum (Plantaray Dark)</li>
    <li> 1 oz light rum (Flor de Cana 4 Extra Seco)</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz lime juice</li>
    <li> 0.5 oz grapefruit juice</li>
    <li> 0.5 oz Demerara syrup</li>
    <li> 0.5 oz apricot liqueur (Rothman & Winter Orchard Apricot)</li>
    <li> 2 dashes Angostura bitters</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with ice and strain into a Collins glass filled with crushed ice.

---

A very pleasant and balanced cocktail. I find using good store-bought grapefruit juice is better here than freshly squeezing some. I think there are two main reasons for it: the consistency of the flavor and brightness of the bitterness. Freshly squeezed juice tends to be sweeter and often lacks punchy bitterness of the grapefruit, which really helps this cocktail's balance. Alternatively, one can try swapping lime + grapefruit juices with 0.75-1 oz of grapefruit superjuice.
