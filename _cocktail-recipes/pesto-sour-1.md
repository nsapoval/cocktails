I am not satisfied with the vast majority of gin basil smashes, so I wanted something a bit more fun and creative.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1 oz light aged rum (Cruzan)</li>
    <li> 1 oz gin</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz orgeat</li>
    <li> 1 oz lemon juice</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz Cynar (70 Proof)</li>
    <li> 6 medium sized basil leaves</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with ice and double strain into a chilled cocktail coupe. Garnish with a basil leaf.

---

This one came out pretty balanced and decently basil-y. I think Cynar really helps with giving more vegetal backbone and depth. Lemon juice/orgeat ration might have to be rebalanced based on how sweet your orgeat is. 

Rating: 8.5 / 10
