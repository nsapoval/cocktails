I wanted to make something that really made pandan and agricole combo shine. This is it, it slaps! Somewhat on a sweeter side, but we all need a treat once in a while.

---

<ul>
    <li> 2 oz rhum agricole blanc</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz [pandan syrup](/cocktails/syrups-recipes/pandan-syrup/)</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz Falernum</li>
    <li> 1 oz coconut water</li>
</ul>

Stir all ingredients in a small glass, garnish with a lime cheek for an optional acidity boost. More coconut water can be added turning this into a long drink.

---

Rating: 9.5 / 10
