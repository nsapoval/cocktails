Finding uses for grapefruit cordial and butterfly pea tequila besides the <a href="https://nsapoval.github.io/cocktails/cocktail-recipes/hollow-purple/">Hollow Purple</a> cocktail. Originally, I was aiming for a Paper Plane-like, equal parts cocktail here, but ended up liking this version more.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1.5 oz <a href="https://nsapoval.github.io/cocktails/infusions-recipes/butterfly-pea-tequila/">butterfly pea infused tequila</a></li>
    <li> 1 oz <a href="https://nsapoval.github.io/cocktails/syrups-recipes/grapefruit-cordial/">grapefruit cordial</a></li>
    <li> 1 oz Amaro Nonino</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with ice and double strain into a Nick and Nora glass.

---

The cocktail turns out balanced with a candy-like acidity and light bitterness from the amaro and grapefruit. You can skip the butterfly pea dying of tequila, but the color won't be as pretty and popping in that case.
