Another recipe from Smuggler's Cove. Tried it relatively recently and decided to add it to my Tiki rotation.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1 oz dark rum (Plantaray Dark)</li>
    <li> 1 oz light rum (Flor de Cana 4 Extra Seco)</li>
    <li> 1 oz lemon juice</li>
    <li> 1 oz cream of coconut</li>
    <li> 1 oz apricot liqueur (Rothman & Winter Orchard Apricot)</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with ice and strain into a Collins glass filled with crushed ice. A blown out paper umbrella is the traditional garnish.

---

I lowered the amount of cream of coconut to 1 oz instead of 1.5 oz in the Smuggler's Cove book recipe. I think the end result is plenty sweet and balanced, but it's not a bad idea to try exploring what amount of cream of coconut suits your taste.
