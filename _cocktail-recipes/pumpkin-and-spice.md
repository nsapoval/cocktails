I wanted to make a riff on the PSL this October while putting a heavy accent on the one flavor absent from the actual PSL: pumpkin. This cocktail went through many iterations until I finally settled on this recipe. I am pretty happy with how it turned out and will likely make it for the Fall season in the future. 

---

<ul>
    <li> 0.75 oz dark Demerara rum (Diamond reserve)</li>
    <li> 0.50 oz Jamaican pot still black rum (Hamilton)</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz Jamaican rum (Smith & Cross)</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz coffee liqueur (Mr. Black)</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz Cynar 70</li>
    <li> 1 tbsp <a href="https://nsapoval.github.io/cocktails/syrups-recipes/sweetened-pumpkin-puree/">sweetened pumpkin puree</a></li>
    <li> 1 oz good quality full fat milk </li>
    <li> 1/4 tsp <a href="https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/pumpkin-pie-spice/">pumpkin pie spice mix</a></li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with ice and double strain into a chilled cocktail glass. Serve up with no garnish.

---

Pumpkin flavor comes through in this cocktail towards the end of every sip. A touch of Cynar adds both complexity and just the right amount of bitterness to balance out what otherwise is a boozy milkshake. Molasses heavy rum and a touch of funk play well with coffee. This cocktail definitely has layers of flavor that support each other. 

Rating: 9.75 / 10
