Grapefruit cordial works great in a gimlet. I made the original one in the photo with a butterfly pea infused tequila, but a butterfly pea infused gin would work really well here too.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1.5 oz <a href="https://nsapoval.github.io/cocktails/infusions-recipes/butterfly-pea-tequila/">butterfly pea infused tequila</a></li>
    <li> 1.5 oz <a href="https://nsapoval.github.io/cocktails/syrups-recipes/grapefruit-cordial/">grapefruit cordial</a></li>
</ul>

Stir all ingredients with ice and double strain into a Nick and Nora glass.

---

Good punchy gimlet with grapefruit flavor.
