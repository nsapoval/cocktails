Another recipe from the <a href="https://www.wonkpress.com/products/minimalist-tiki">Minimalist Tiki</a> book. This one features bourbon in a Tiki cocktail for a good balance and twist.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1.50 oz high-proof bourbon (Wild Turkey 101)</li>
    <li> 0.50 oz Planteray Stiggins' Fancy Pineapple Rum</li>
    <li> 1 oz lemon juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz pineapple juice</li>
    <li> 0.50 oz orgeat</li>
    <li> 0.50 oz orange liqueur (Grand Marnier)</li>
    <li> 0.25 oz cinnamon syrup</li>
    <li> 3 drops Bittermens 'Elemakule Tiki Bitters</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with crushed ice (or flash blend). Pour into a double old-fashioned glass. Top off with fresh crushed ice. Garnish with a mint sprig, orchid and a pineapple wedge.

---

Swapped Tiki Bitters for 2/1 ratio of Angostura and Peychauds. The combination of pineapple, spices and liquors really works here and ties out for a very balanced cocktail. 

Rating: 9.5 / 10
