This recipe marks the start of my journey through the <a href="https://www.wonkpress.com/products/minimalist-tiki">Minimalist Tiki</a> book. 

---

<ul>
    <li> 1.5 oz Planteray O.F.T.D Overproof rum</li>
    <li> 1.5 oz aged dark rum (Planteray Original Dark)</li>
    <li> 1.5 oz grapefruit juice</li>
    <li> 0.75 oz lime juice</li>
    <li> 0.50 oz orgeat</li>
    <li> 0.50 oz cinnamon syrup</li>
    <li> 0.50 oz allspice dram</li>
    <li> 0.50 oz Falernum</li>
</ul>

Shake all ingredients with crushed ice (or flash blend). Pour into a large snifter or a stemless wine glass. Top off with fresh crushed ice. Garnish with a mint sprig.

---

Nicely spiced and strong, although for my taste could use a touch more acidity (either taking lime to 1 oz or maybe mixing 0.25 oz grapefruit super juice and 1.25 oz regular grapefruit juice). Nonetheless a great tiki drink that I'm planning to make again. 

Rating: 8 / 10
