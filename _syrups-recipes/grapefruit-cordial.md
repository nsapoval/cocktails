Essentially mixing and matching the ideas from <a href="https://jeffreymorgenthaler.com/lime-cordial/">Jeffrey Morgenthaler's lime cordial</a> and <a href="https://www.kevinkos.com/post/grapefruit-super-juice">Kevin Kos's Grapefruit Superjuice</a> recipes.

---

<ul>
    <li> ~8 g grapefruit zest (microplaned) </li>
    <li> 250 g sugar</li>
    <li> 20 g citric acid</li>
    <li> 5 g malic acid</li>
    <li> 1/8 tsp MSG</li>
    <li> 2 oz grapefruit juice</li>
    <li> 8 oz water</li>
</ul>

Heat up the water (I use microwave for about 2 minutes) and then blend all ingredients in a blender. Strain into a bottle and keep refrigerated.
