I sometimes find myself with a half full bottle of red wine in the fridge a day or two past its peak. Typically it's good enough that I don't want to dump it, and I might not have a recipe calling for red wine based sauce at the moment. Hence, I've been thinking about whether I can make a syrup out of it. I recommend using a bold big red for this.

---

<ul>
    <li> 12 oz red wine</li>
    <li> 0.5 tbsp pomegranate molasses</li>
    <li> 1 barspoon rose water</li>
    <li> 0.75 cups sugar</li>
</ul>

Heat up the wine on the stove and keep simmering until reduced by half. The goal is to go from 12 oz of wine to 6 oz of wine reduction. Then add molasses and sugar and stir (or blend) to combine and dissolve, the mixture will be warm form reduction so sugar will dissolve easily. Let cool, add rose water and bottle. Should keep in the fridge about as well as any other 1:1 sugar syrup.
