<a href=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pandanus_amaryllifolius>Pandan</a> has a beautiful sweet aroma that is somewhat reminiscent of vanilla. It is generally hard to describe exactly, but it is an unmistakable fragrance once you get familiar with it. 

---

<ul>
    <li> 50-55 g frozen pandan leaves</li>
    <li> 2 cups sugar</li>
    <li> 1 cup water</li>
</ul>

Heat up water and start dissolving sugar in it, when sugar is partially dissolved add pandan and kill the heat. Once the syrup is clear let it sit for another 15-30 minutes and then remove the leaves and bottle.
