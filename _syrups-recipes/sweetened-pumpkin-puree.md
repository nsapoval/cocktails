This is a simple recipe for turning a pumpkin or a winter squash into a nice sweetened puree for cocktails. I used blue Hubbard squash, and from my experience with winter squash varieties I would suggest sticking to it as it offers good balance of pumpkin flavor and easy to work with texture.

---

<ul>
    <li> 1.5 cups roasted pumpkin</li>
    <li> 1 cups 2:1 syrup</li>
</ul>

Peel and core the pumpkin and cut it into 1.5 inch cubes/chunks. Bake the pumpkin in an oven at 425 F for about 45 minutes. Optionally turn on broiler for the last few minutes to give a bit of char to the pumpkin bits. Then blend the pumpkin with 2:1 syrup until a smooth puree forms. Will keep in the fridge for a few weeks. Easiest to measure out in spoons as the puree is not liquid enough to be easily poured.
